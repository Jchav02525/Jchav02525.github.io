I'm a 20 year old female with a bit of professional experience in a number of areas (babysitting young children, looking after animals, making flowers etc). The experience has given me a good grounding in a number of skills which I feel have been instrumental in my personal development and in preparing me for a career. I would describe myself as a hard working, responsible, creative and dedicated individual who is always up for a challenge and is keen to learn. I've had plenty of experience in the babysitting field and it's taught me a great deal about patience, responsibility and the need to be on your toes. Looking after children is all about being attentive, being dependable and being able to deal with all sorts of situations. You have to be able to create a safe and positive environment and at the same time look after the kids and make them happy. I've also had experience in looking after animals and that's taught me a lot about responsibility, patience and consistency. Whether I'm looking after kids or animals, the principle is the same really, you just have to be there and give them the attention and care they need. Another area in which I have gained professional experience is through my own floral business. I've been self employed in the creation of beautiful floral arrangements for all sorts of occasions. It's given me the opportunity to be creative and has taught me a great deal about running a business. Starting your own business in itself is a great lesson in dedication, time management, customer service and attention to detail. I take great pride in the work that I do and enjoy the process of taking someone else's idea and bringing it to life. Running my own business has also taught me the importance of being organised, of communicating with customers and of ensuring that you deliver quality work. In addition to my professional experience I am also a collegiate athlete having both competed and continuing to compete at the D1 level. Being a college athlete has in fact taught me some of the most valuable life lessons that I think I can take with me into any career. It's taught me the value of showing up, being on time, being committed, and just putting in the work when things get tough. Juggling school, sport and everything else has given me a real appreciation for time management and has taught me how to stay committed to what you're after. Being a Division 1 athlete has also instilled in me the value of teamwork and communication. You really do have to work with others, you have to listen, you have to support the people around you and you have to give your all. You learn that success doesn't come overnight and it takes a lot of consistency, a lot of discipline and a positive attitude to get where you want to go. There will be hurdles along the way, but I've learned how to stay motivated and how to keep pushing. Overall I think my experiences have made me a reliable, hardworking, creative sort of person who is willing to put in the work to get things done. I'm always looking for opportunities to grow, to learn and to have new experiences. The skills I've acquired through my work, my floral business and as a collegiate athlete I'm confident can be applied to any professional setting. I take pride in what I do and I always aim to give my best.
